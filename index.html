Nothing happens? Read this
  • Location: allow it when the browser asks. On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Safari Websites.
  • Motion (steps): iPhone asks once when you tap Start. If you said no, close the tab and open the page again.
  • Keep the screen on. iPhone pauses GPS when the screen is off. Route Lab asks the phone to stay awake while you walk.
  • Go outside. Indoors, GPS can be 20 m off or missing. Wait until the accuracy chip says 10 m or better.
  • No sensors (a laptop)? Use Demo walk to watch a simulated walk, or +1 test step to count steps by hand.
